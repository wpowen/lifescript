Tianjilu: Fate Codex

An immersive interactive cultivation novel where every choice reshapes destiny. Scheme, foresee, and rewrite the laws of fate.

About

Tianjilu is a premium, offline-first interactive fiction app. You play as Chen Ji, a seemingly worthless disciple who discovers a fragmented fate-reading scroll. Every decision you make — whom to trust, when to strike, how much destiny to spend — changes the story.

Features

  • 1,200 chapters of branching narrative
  • Seven-dimensional character attributes
  • Multi-axis relationship tracking
  • Chapter-end settlement and consequence review
  • Cinematic dark-mode reading UI
  • 4 languages: Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean
