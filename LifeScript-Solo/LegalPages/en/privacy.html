Privacy Policy

Tianjilu is an offline-first interactive reading app. No account is required, and we do not rely on ads, user profiling, or third-party tracking.

1. What We Process

The current version does not collect phone numbers, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or location data. The app only stores reading-related information locally on your device.

  • Local reading progress and recent chapters
  • Reading preferences such as font size and motion settings
  • Local chapter choice records for continuation and settlement

2. How We Use This Information

Local data is used solely to support features you actively triggered. It is never used for ad targeting, user profiling, or sold to third parties.

  • Resume where you left off
  • Preserve character relationships, stats, and chapter outcomes
  • Avoid repeated reading preference setup

3. Sharing, Deletion & Inquiries

The current version does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or account system, so local reading data is not routinely shared. If you have privacy inquiries or wish to request data deletion assistance, please contact us by email.
