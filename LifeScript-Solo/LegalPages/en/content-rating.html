Content Rating

Tianjilu is an interactive cultivation novel containing fantasy themes intended for mature readers. Please review the following before continuing.

Content Includes

  • Fantasy violence: combat, pursuits, and battle scenes
  • Blood and injury descriptions in a fantasy context
  • Death, sacrifice, and imprisonment of fictional characters
  • Dark themes: betrayal, psychological pressure, and threats
  • Moral ambiguity: player choices determine who survives or suffers

Content Does NOT Include

  • No explicit sexual content or nudity
  • No real-world violence or references to real people/events
  • No user-generated content
  • No account system or social features
  • No drug, alcohol, or tobacco references

Recommended Age: 17+

Due to the intensity of fantasy violence and dark narrative themes, this app is recommended for readers aged 17 and above. Parental discretion is advised.
